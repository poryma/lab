About the lab

South Korea has the highest suicide rate in the OECD, about forty deaths a day, and every year more people here die with no one beside them and are found days or weeks later. Behind those numbers are patterns. Age, gender, region, work, family, and neighborhood all shape who is at risk. We try to make those patterns visible, carefully and without losing sight of the people inside the data.

The lab sits where sociology meets social welfare and social policy. Sociology helps us explain why some people are at greater risk. Welfare and policy ask what to do about it: which services reach people, how to find isolated older adults in time, and which local prevention efforts work. We want our findings to be useful to the people doing that work, not only to other researchers.
