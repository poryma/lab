Work with us

Students, collaborators, and practitioners are all welcome. Here's how to reach us.

Who we're looking for

  • Undergraduates in Public Sociology who want research experience, a thesis topic, or a path to graduate school.
  • Graduate students in Social Welfare (master's and doctoral) interested in suicide, mental health, isolation, or prevention policy.
  • Collaborators and practitioners: researchers, welfare and prevention workers, and public agencies who want to work with data on these questions.

You don't need to arrive knowing Stata or Python. You do need curiosity, patience with messy data, and respect for the subject. Social work and policy experience is as welcome as statistics. This research involves reading about people's deaths, and we look after each other while doing it.

How to apply

There's no form. Send one email to poryma@korea.ac.kr with the subject line "[Lab] Your name - program - topic", and include:

  1. Who you are: your program and year (or your organization).
  2. What you want to study, and why: a few sentences is enough. A question you can't stop thinking about is better than a polished topic.
  3. What you bring: courses, methods, languages, field or work experience. Nothing is too small to mention.
  4. What you're hoping for: a research assistant role, thesis advising, graduate study, or a collaboration.
  5. Attachments (optional): a CV or transcript, and a writing sample if you have one.

Please read our AI principles first; they apply to everyone working with us. Write your email yourself. It doesn't need to be perfect English (or Korean). I'd rather hear how you actually think.
